# Dagannoth Kings

[Open Dagannoth Kings simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/dagannoth-kings/) · [All simulators](../README.md)

Enter separate kill counts for **Dagannoth Rex**, **Dagannoth Prime**, and **Dagannoth Supreme**. Their loot inventories, unique rewards, and dry-calculator results are all displayed separately, rather than combined.

Each kill produces **Dagannoth bones** and **Dagannoth hide** plus one exclusive 128-ticket main-table roll. Rare/gem, talisman, and rare-seed subtables occupy main-table tickets instead of independent drops. Quantities are rolled across inclusive ranges.

| King | Main uniques (each 1/128) | Independent pet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rex | Berserker ring, Warrior ring, Dragon axe | Pet Dagannoth Rex, 1/5,000 |
| Prime | Seers ring, Mud battlestaff, Dragon axe | Pet Dagannoth Prime, 1/5,000 |
| Supreme | Archers ring, Seercull, Dragon axe | Pet Dagannoth Supreme, 1/5,000 |

Independent tertiary rewards per kill include an ensouled dagannoth head (1/20), hard clue scroll (1/42 or 1/39 with Hard Combat Achievements), elite clue scroll (1/750 or 1/712 with Elite Combat Achievements), and each king's pet. Optional Konar Slayer tasks add brimstone keys (1/60); the Rag and Bone Man II option adds dagannoth ribs (1/4).

The dry calculator uses three mutually exclusive main uniques at a combined 3/128 rate and one independent pet at 1/5,000. The *any rare reward* calculation correctly permits a main unique and a pet from the same kill. Values and dry results are tracked separately for the kings, up to 1,000,000 kills each.

Rare/gem and seed subtable probabilities use normalized published rounded rates. The simulator does not track inventory restrictions on clue scrolls, ring-of-wealth rare-table boosts, Legends' Quest modifiers, or diary-noted bones.

Sources: [Rex](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dagannoth_Rex), [Prime](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dagannoth_Prime), [Supreme](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dagannoth_Supreme).
