# Elite Clue Caskets

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/treasure-trails/elite/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [Elite rewards](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Reward_casket_%28elite%29) · [Mimic rewards](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Mimic#Elite_drops)

Enter normal elite caskets and elite Mimic caskets separately. Each casket belongs in one field; both counts contribute to the combined result.

- Every casket gives 4–6 regular elite rolls and a separate 1/5 master clue chance. Mimics also give one bonus reward and a mahogany plank, assuming first-attempt wins.
- Tracks all 98 elite collection-log items plus six additional 3rd Age items available from Mimics. Shared clue rewards appear in simulated loot.
- Dry odds combine regular rolls and Mimic bonuses without averaging their probabilities. Mimic-only rewards are impossible with zero Mimic caskets.
- Assumes master clues can be claimed each time. Milestone scroll cases and the heavy casket are excluded. The entered Mimic count is used directly.
- Supports up to 1,000,000 combined caskets. Images are local; negligible probability tails are trimmed for large dry calculations.
