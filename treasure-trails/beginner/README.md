# Beginner Clue Caskets

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/treasure-trails/beginner/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Reward_casket_%28beginner%29)

Enter opened caskets. Each gives 1–3 equally likely reward rolls; duplicates can occur within one casket.

- Includes all ordinary loot, 15 cosmetics at 1/360 each per roll, and the black pickaxe at 11/8,856 per roll.
- Dry odds track all 16 collection-log items and their total, retaining the random roll count rather than assuming two rolls per casket. Negligible probability tails are trimmed for large inputs.
- Assumes an account eligible for uniques. Milestone scroll cases and the Explore emote are excluded. Images are served locally; up to 1,000,000 caskets are supported.
