# Master Clue Caskets

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/treasure-trails/master/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [Master rewards](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Reward_casket_%28master%29) · [Mimic rewards](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Mimic#Master_drops)

Enter normal master caskets and master Mimic caskets separately. Count each casket once; both inputs contribute to the combined result.

- Normal caskets give 5–7 reward rolls. Mimics assume first-attempt wins: six regular rolls, one bonus reward and a mahogany plank.
- Both types independently roll for Bloodhound at 1/1,000 per casket. All 94 collection-log items, including the pet, contribute to the combined dry result.
- Includes ordinary loot and shared clue rewards. Uses exact table probabilities, including both sources of god pages and the extra coin outcome.
- Mimic counts are used directly. Milestone scroll cases and the scroll sack are excluded.
- Supports up to 1,000,000 combined caskets. Images are local; negligible probability tails are trimmed for large dry calculations.
