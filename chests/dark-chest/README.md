# Dark Chest

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/chests/dark-chest/) · [Chest guides](../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dark_Chest)

Shield left half, dragon plateskirt and dragon platelegs each have a 1/600 chance. The three rare outcomes are mutually exclusive.

- A successful rare pre-roll replaces the ordinary 600-ticket reward table.
- The dry calculator tracks these three rare rewards.
