# Zalcano

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/zalcano/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Zalcano)

Enter kills, team size and your shield/HP contribution. At least five shield damage is required for loot; at least 31 combined damage is required for rares.

- MVP increases common quantities by 10% and awards extra guaranteed loot. XP-focused mode skips ordinary resources while keeping eligible rares.
- Dry odds use your contribution and eligibility, preserving the shared tool/onyx roll. Quantities and team allocation follow the modeled contribution rather than assuming every team rare belongs to you.
