# Zalcano

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/zalcano/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Zalcano)

Combine Trios (one-third contribution, MVP every third Trio kill) and Mass (minimum rare-eligible contribution: 5 shield + 26 HP damage). Mass never receives MVP rewards.

- Smolcano stays 1/2,250; minimum-contribution Zalcano shard is 1/1,500. Tool seed and onyx share one team roll.
- Presets assume a 1,000-point team baseline. Allocation is estimated proportionally; resource quantities and shard rates interpolate published endpoints because exact formulas are unpublished.
- Dry odds combine both presets separately. Up to 1,000,000 combined kills are supported.
