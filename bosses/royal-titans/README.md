# Royal Titans

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/royal-titans/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Royal_Titans)

Enter Solo and Duo kill counts, choose a Titan to loot for each, and select Loot, Sacrifice or Take pages.

- Loot rolls uniques before ordinary rewards; Sacrifice forfeits primary loot to double the pet chance; Take pages gives pages instead of primary loot.
- Dry odds combine contribution and reward choices. Published marginal unique chances are modeled as mutually exclusive outcomes because their exact internal order is not documented. Clues remain available for all choices.
