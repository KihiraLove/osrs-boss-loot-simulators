# Yama

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/yama/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Yama)

Enter Solo (100%) and Duo (50%) kills together. This models ordinary encounters, without contracts.

- Lockboxes and dossiers are opened immediately and shown separately. The first eligible dossier at or after 100 combined kills guarantees the rite, assuming one continuous character history.
- Dry odds combine the two contribution rates. Enter actual oathplate shard quantities: 12 per solo drop, six per duo drop. Container contents and forgotten lockboxes are excluded from dry inputs.
