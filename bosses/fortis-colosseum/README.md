# Fortis Colosseum

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/fortis-colosseum/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Rewards_Chest_%28Fortis_Colosseum%29)

Enter completed runs and the highest cleared wave. Each run earns rewards from all completed waves; unique rewards replace the ordinary reward for their wave.

- Wave 12 gives Dizana's quiver. Choose to keep it, exchange it for splinters, or gamble it for a pet chance. Gambling stops after the simulated pet is obtained.
- The dry calculator follows wave-specific chances and quiver choices. Glory affects common loot rather than unique probability. Existing pets and past runs are not tracked.
