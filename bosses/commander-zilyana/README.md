# Commander Zilyana

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/commander-zilyana/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/General_Zilyana) · [Wiki source 2](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Starlight) · [Wiki source 3](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Growler) · [Wiki source 4](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Bree)

Each entered kill includes Commander Zilyana and her three bodyguards. Their loot appears in separate source panels.

- Dry odds combine boss and bodyguard chances, including the possibility of several copies from one room clear.
- Hard and Elite Combat Achievements improve the matching clues. Ring of wealth affects rare-table rewards.
