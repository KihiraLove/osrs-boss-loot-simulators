## Mechanics and drop rates

[Scurrius](./) has a standalone loot simulator and dry calculator for **solo kills**. Each kill uses a **100-ticket** common-loot table and guarantees one big bones and one raw rat meat. Quantity ranges are rolled inclusively.

The only two collection-log rewards are **Scurrius' spine (1/33)** and the **Scurry pet (1/3,000)**. The other independent tertiary rewards are medium clue scroll (1/25, or **1/23** with Medium Combat Achievements), long bone (1/400) and curved bone (1/5,012.5).

The dry calculator accepts the number of received collection-log rewards and reports probabilities of fewer, exactly, and more drops alongside expected quantities and odds of at least one. The total count combines two independently rolled rewards, which can occur on the same kill.

The frontend runs locally, uses bundled PNG item assets, and does not require a runtime Wiki API.

Sources: [OSRS Wiki Scurrius](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Scurrius) and [Scurrius drop table](https://reldo.org/a/scurrius/).
