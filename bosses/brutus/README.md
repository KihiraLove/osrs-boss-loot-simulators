# Brutus

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/brutus/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Brutus)

Enter post-quest Brutus kills and choose Members or Free-to-play rewards.

- Mooleta: 5/150 on either world type. Members can also receive the bottomless milk bucket at 4/150, cow slippers at 1/150 and Beef at 1/1,000.
- The first three rewards share one replacing roll; Beef rolls separately. Dry odds preserve this distinction. Easy Combat Achievements improve easy clues.
