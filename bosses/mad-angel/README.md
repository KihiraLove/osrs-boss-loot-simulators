# Mad Angel

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/mad-angel/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Mad_Angel)

Enter repeatable post-quest kills. Each gives granite dust; the optional Sunstone crystal is awarded only once.

- Ardeaglais teleports, Hallowfell and Jar of Light use one mutually exclusive replacing roll at their published probabilities. Aggy rolls separately at 1/2,000.
- Supply rewards arrive as bundles. Dry odds preserve teleport quantities and independent pet rolls. Medium Combat Achievements and Ring of wealth are supported.
