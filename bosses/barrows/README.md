# Barrows

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/barrows/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Chest_(Barrows))

Enter chests opened after defeating all six brothers. Each chest has seven attempts at the 24 equipment pieces; duplicate pieces within one chest are discarded.

- A named piece has probability 1 − (1 − 1/2,448)^7 per chest. The dry calculator accounts for duplicate suppression.
- Reward potential is fixed at 880. Common stack quantities are interpolated estimates. Hard Morytania Diary boosts runes; Elite Combat Achievements improve clues.
