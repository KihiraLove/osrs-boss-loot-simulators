# Vet'ion &amp; Calvar'ion

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/vetion-calvarion/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Vet'ion) · [Wiki source 2](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Calvar'ion)

Enter Vet'ion and Calvar'ion kills separately to combine their loot and dry results.

- Each boss retains its own rare and pet probabilities. Mixed dry odds combine both distributions instead of averaging their rates.
- Both encounters use solo/full-credit rewards. Ring of wealth (i) affects Wilderness clue rolls and rare-table loot.
