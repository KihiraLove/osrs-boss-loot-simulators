# Barbarian Assault High Gambles

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/barbarian-assault/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Barbarian_Assault/Gambles#High_Gambles)

Enter completed high gambles. Includes all main rewards, dragon armour, the Penance Queen pet and elite clues.

- Pet: 1/1,000 per gamble. Elite clue: 1/15, improving to 1/14 with elite Combat Achievements. Both roll independently of main loot.
- Dry odds track granite helms, dragon chainbodies, dragon med helms, pets and clues. The combined collection-log result includes granite helms, dragon chainbodies and pets.
- The Wiki lists 32 common outcomes plus dragon armour at 1/16,000 each. Dragon armour is modeled as an exclusive pre-roll; common outcomes share the remaining probability equally.
- Shop purchases and point earnings are excluded. Supports up to 1,000,000 gambles. Images are local.
