## Mechanics and drop rates

The **Steel ring**, introduced on 25 June 2025, is this boss's only collection-log unique. It occupies **3 of 131 exclusive main-table tickets** (**3/131, approximately 1/43.67 per kill**). Receiving it replaces the regular main drop rather than being an additional tertiary reward. Every kill guarantees one bones.

The complete **131-ticket** main table includes the published weapon, rune, material, consumable and other rewards, with their quantities, plus **1/131 tree/herb seed**, **6/131 rare**, and **6/131 gem** branches. The seed and rare/gem secondary rolls use the OSRS Wiki's rounded published overall chances, normalized without rescaling the main-table tickets. Ring-of-wealth boosts and Legends' Quest rare-table modifiers are not modeled.

Independent tertiary rolls include **Numulite (5–32) at 108/175**, and one exclusive fossil outcome: small **12/350**, medium **12/700**, large **12/875**, or rare **12/3,500**. An **elite clue scroll** rolls separately at **1/200**, improving to **1/190** with the **Elite Combat Achievements** option. Optional **Konar Slayer task** kills can also award a **brimstone key at 1/65**. Clue-scroll inventory restrictions are not simulated.

The dry calculator takes received Steel rings and reports fewer/exactly/more probabilities, expected quantity and at-least-one odds using a binomial distribution. Loot sprites are stored locally.

Source: [OSRS Wiki — Deranged archaeologist](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Deranged_archaeologist).
