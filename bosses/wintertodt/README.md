# Wintertodt

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/wintertodt/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Reward_Cart)

Enter Reward Cart pulls and your skills when claiming rewards. This uses the October 2024 Reward Cart, not old Supply crates.

- Rare checks stop at the first success. Material quantities depend on the relevant skill. Pyromancer pieces follow least-owned order with fixed tie-breaking.
- Sessions start with no owned items. Gloves and torches cap at three; later hits become seeds. Dry odds account for these caps and the outfit sequence.
