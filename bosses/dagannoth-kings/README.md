# Dagannoth Kings

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/dagannoth-kings/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Dagannoth_Kings)

Enter Rex, Prime and Supreme kills separately. Each king has its own loot and dry-result panels.

- Each king has three main uniques at 1/128 each and a separate pet at 1/5,000. A main unique and pet can arrive together.
- Hard and Elite Combat Achievements affect clues. Ring of wealth affects rare and gem tables. Existing clue ownership and diary-noted bones are not tracked.
