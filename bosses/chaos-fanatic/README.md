# Chaos Fanatic

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/chaos-fanatic/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Chaos_Fanatic)

Enter completed kills to simulate loot or calculate dry odds for shard pieces and the pet.

- Shard outcomes share a roll; the pet rolls independently. The combined result accounts for both.
- Ring of wealth (i) includes normal ring effects and doubles Wilderness clues. Hard Combat Achievements improve hard clues.
