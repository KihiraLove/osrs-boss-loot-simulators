# Crazy Archaeologist

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/crazy-archaeologist/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Crazy_archaeologist)

Enter completed Crazy Archaeologist kills. The simulator includes ordinary loot, shard pieces, the fedora and clues.

- Dry odds preserve shared-table outcomes and independent rewards when combining your received counts.
- Ring of wealth (i) includes normal ring effects and doubles Wilderness clues. Hard Combat Achievements improve hard clues.
