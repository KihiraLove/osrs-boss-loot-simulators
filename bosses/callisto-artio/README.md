# Callisto &amp; Artio

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/callisto-artio/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Callisto) · [Wiki source 2](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Artio)

Enter Callisto and Artio kills separately to combine their loot and dry results.

- Each boss keeps its own unique and pet rates. Mixed dry odds combine the two distributions instead of averaging their rates.
- Both encounters are solo/full-credit models. Ring of wealth (i) affects Wilderness clue rolls and rare-table loot.
