# Skotizo

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/skotizo/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Skotizo)

Each kill gives 1–5 ancient shards, a hard clue and one ordinary loot roll, with independent rare rewards.

- Totem outcomes share one roll. Pieces are assigned to the least-owned type, with ties resolved base → middle → top. Starting inventory is empty; totems are not assembled or reused.
- Dry odds cover Dark claws, Skotos, Jar of Darkness, uncut onyx and complete dark totems. Elite Combat Achievements and Ring of wealth are supported.
