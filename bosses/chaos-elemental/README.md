# Chaos Elemental

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/chaos-elemental/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Chaos_Elemental)

Enter completed Chaos Elemental kills to simulate loot or compare your rare rewards.

- The dragon-pickaxe pre-roll replaces ordinary loot; the pet and clue roll separately. Dry totals allow independent rare rewards on the same kill.
- Ring of wealth (i) includes the normal ring effect and doubles Wilderness clue chances. Elite Combat Achievements also improve elite clues.
