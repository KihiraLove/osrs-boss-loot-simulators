# Doom of Mokhaiotl

[Open simulator](https://kihiralove.github.io/osrs-boss-loot-simulators/bosses/doom/) · [All simulators](../../README.md) · [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Doom_of_Mokhaiotl)

Enter individual completed delves at each level. A run through level 8 needs one entry at every level from 1 to 8. The 9+ field counts individual deeper delves at level-9 rates.

- Each delve gives its own common loot, demon tears and eligible rare rolls. Cloth, Eye of ayak and treads share an equipment roll; Dom rolls independently.
- Dry odds combine the different level rates. Individual cards are per-item probabilities; the combined card counts all four rares. Deaths, unclaimed loot and existing account progress are not modeled.
